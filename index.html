Privacy & storage

Trades are stored in IndexedDB on this device. Nothing is sent to a trading broker or cloud service.

Important notes

  • Enter quantity as total units, not number of lots.
  • Net P&L = (Exit price − Entry price) × quantity − charges. This assumes a long option position; for short positions, use the short-position toggle in a future enhancement or record the trade as a positive buy/sell direction in your notes.
  • Keep a separate backup regularly. Browser data can be cleared by device cleanup or browser settings.
  • Each device has its own local database. Use export on one device and restore on another to move data; this is not automatic sync.
